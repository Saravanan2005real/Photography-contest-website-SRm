Context

About Frames 360

Frames 360: Design for Future is a photography, videography, and poster design competition that encourages students to communicate meaningful social and environmental stories through visual media.

Participants are invited to create original photographs, videos, or poster designs inspired by any one of the 17 United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs). The objective is to raise awareness, inspire action, and present innovative perspectives on building a sustainable future.

Themes

Design for Future

Choose any Sustainable Development Goal (SDG) and represent its significance through:

Photography

Capture a single, powerful frame that tells an evocative story, freezes a crucial moment of change, or highlights local sustainability issues and solutions.

  • High Resolution Images
  • Single Frame storytelling
  • Concept and clarity focus

Videography

Produce a short, high-impact video, reel, or documentary presenting a message, solution, or critical narrative around your chosen goal.

  • Dynamic storytelling edits
  • High-quality audio and visuals
  • Clear narrative structure

Poster Design

Design a compelling and creative poster that communicates a strong message related to your chosen SDG, combining visuals and typography effectively.

  • Creative typography and visuals
  • Strong message delivery
  • High-resolution design

Rules

Guidelines & Eligibility

Submission Rules

  • 1

    Originality: All entries must be captured/recorded by the participant. Plagiarism will lead to immediate disqualification.

  • 2

    SDG Representation: Visual entries must represent the UN Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs).

  • 3

    Strict Deadline: Submissions must be uploaded before 10 August 2026. Late uploads cannot be accommodated.
